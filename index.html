🧠 How WATTWISE Works

1. Single Point Measurement
The system measures the total electricity consumption of the household from a single point.

2. Signal Processing
The collected aggregate power signal is cleaned and prepared for analysis.

3. Deep Learning
CNN/LSTM models can learn appliance-specific patterns from the aggregate signal.

4. Appliance Disaggregation
The system estimates how much energy individual appliances are consuming.

5. Cost Estimation
Energy consumption in kWh is multiplied by the configured electricity tariff.
